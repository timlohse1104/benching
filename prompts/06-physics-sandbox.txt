Build a self-contained HTML page with a 2D physics sandbox of bouncing balls on a Canvas.

Requirements:
- Single .html file. Inline CSS and JS only. No external resources.
- A full-window <canvas> animated via requestAnimationFrame.
- Balls of varying radius that fall under gravity, bounce off the walls and floor with energy loss (restitution), and collide with each other (circle-circle collision response).
- Click on the canvas to spawn a new ball; click-drag to fling it with an initial velocity based on the drag vector.
- A control panel to adjust at least three parameters live: gravity, restitution (bounciness), and friction.
- Buttons to reset the scene and to toggle collisions between balls on/off.
- Balls have distinct colors; show a live count of active balls.
- Keep the simulation stable with dozens of balls (no runaway energy or tunneling through walls).
- Use a clean dark theme.
- Must be valid HTML5. Must not throw any JavaScript errors when loaded.
- Output ONLY the HTML. No markdown fences, no commentary, no explanation.
